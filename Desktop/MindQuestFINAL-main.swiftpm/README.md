# MindQuest 🧠✨
### Elevating the Human Experience through Emotional Intelligence

MindQuest is a premium, multi-agent wellness companion designed to transform the way we interact with our mental health. It combines advanced behavioral psychology with immersive technology to provide a sanctuary for focus, recovery, and personal growth — all within a lightweight 15MB footprint.

---

## 💎 The Vision: Why MindQuest?
Most wellness apps feel like chores or static data logs. **MindQuest** was built on the principle of **"Tactile Intelligence."** We believe that mental health tracking should feel alive, responsive, and supportive. Every interaction is designed to provide emotional warmth, from the time-aware greetings to the high-fidelity haptic feedback that celebrates your smallest victories.

---

## 🧠 The Multi-Agent Architecture
The app is powered by a sophisticated suite of **14+ Autonomous Agents** that coordinate to personalize your experience without complex cloud infra:

*   **SmartWelcomeAgent:** Adapts greetings and subtitles based on time of day and your last emotional state.
*   **EngagementAgent:** Manages a multi-tier XP system, streaks, and milestone detection.
*   **HapticAgent:** A production-grade tactile engine providing 5 impact styles and complex compound patterns.
*   **MotionAgent:** Orchestrates micro-animations (bounces, shakes, bursts) to elevate the premium feel.
*   **CognitiveInsightAgent:** Analyzes your behavioral patterns to provide localized smart insights.
*   **StabilityAgent:** Ensures performance safety by deduplicating haptics and managing audio lifecycles.
*   **FocusAgent:** Automatically detects deep focus sessions and awards badges for mental endurance.
*   **Hydration & Wellness Agents:** Track consistency in physical habits (water, sleep, exercise).

## ✨ Feature Deep-Dive

### 1. Emotional Intelligence Layer
- **Mood Tracking & Analysis:** High-resolution sparkline visualizations and a comprehensive calendar view.
- **Adaptive UI:** The entire interface shifts its gradient and ambient particle drift to match your dominant mood.
- **Daily Intent:** A dedicated spark of mindfulness to set your tone every morning.

### 2. Wellness DNA & Identity
- **Wellness DNA Ring:** An animated, 4-layered "genome" ring visualizing your balance between Sleep, Hydration, Exercise, and Mood.
- **Consistency Velocity:** A real-time meter measuring your pace of self-improvement since joining.
- **Achievement Vault:** 12 hidden achievements with a custom unlocking framework and celebratory haptic patterns.

### 3. Discovery & Surprise
- **Mystery Activity:** A daily "shimmer" card that reveals a random creative or mindful task once tapped.
- **Mini Challenges:** Rotating 3-day challenges designed to build long-term habits without overwhelm.

### 4. Interactive Engines (9 Games)
Fully functional interactive puzzle engines built from scratch with native logic:
- **Spatial:** Maze Generator (Recursive Division), Sliding Puzzles, Number Path (DFS-based).
- **Cognitive:** Word Scramble, Trivia Quiz, Pattern Match, Memory Match.
- **Action:** Bubble Pop (Haptic physics), Reaction Speed Test.

### 5. Procedural Audio Synthesizer
- **Solfeggio Frequencies:** Real-time synthesis of healing frequencies (174Hz to 852Hz) using `AVAudioEngine`.
- **Lightweight:** High-quality soundscapes without the megabytes — procedural generation keeps the app tiny.

## 🎨 Motion & Tactile Intelligence
We use a **Layered Enhancement** philosophy:
- **Micro-Animations:** Emoji bounces, ripple effects, radial completion bursts, and pulsing breathing orbs.
- **Structured Haptics:** Distinct tactile languages for success (`.success`), milestones (`.celebration`), and gentle guidance (`.soft`).
- **Glassmorphism:** Elegant use of `.ultraThinMaterial` and depth-aware shadows for a modern, Apple-native aesthetic.

- **Language:** Swift 6.0 (Strict Concurrency)
- **Framework:** SwiftUI (Purely native, no Lottie, no 3rd-party libs)
- **Size:** **< 15MB** (Optimized for performance and accessibility)
